# Code of Conduct

warpSpeed OPEN is committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and professional developer community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Help newcomers learn.
- Keep discussions technical and professional.
- Give clear feedback without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, abuse, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Spam or repeated self-promotion.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Submitting copied or plagiarised work.

# warpSpeed OPEN Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in warpSpeed OPEN a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Bounty Participation

All bounty participants are expected to:
- Submit original work or properly licensed code
- Respect the claim process and wait for maintainer confirmation
- Meet deadlines and commit milestones as specified in bounty terms
- Respond professionally to code review feedback
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block users who violate these standards.
