## Roles

- New Contributor: A developer exploring or claiming a bounty.
- Contributor: A developer who has submitted accepted work.
- Core Contributor: A trusted contributor with repeated successful work.
- Maintainer: A reviewer who can approve work, manage issues, and merge PRs.

# Governance

## Overview

This repository is maintained by the warpSpeed OPEN team. Bounty tasks are created, reviewed, and managed by designated maintainers.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and approving bounty claims
- Evaluating submissions
- Merging approved pull requests
- Enforcing the Code of Conduct

## Decision Making

- Bounty claims are approved on a first-come, first-served basis for qualified contributors.
- Submission quality is evaluated against the bounty requirements.
- Final decisions on payment eligibility rest with warpSpeed OPEN maintainers.

## Changes to Governance

Proposed changes to governance should be submitted as issues for discussion.
Maintainers review bounty claims, pull requests, and acceptance criteria. Final merge decisions belong to maintainers.
